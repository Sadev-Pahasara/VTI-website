Diploma in Law

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Law is to provide learners with advanced legal knowledge, critical thinking, and analytical skills across various areas of law. This qualification equips learners to evaluate legal issues, apply legal principles, and provide professional legal solutions in complex contexts.

Benefits

Graduates will be well-prepared for careers in legal practice, judiciary, public administration, or corporate legal advisory, and progression to LLM or MBA top-up programmes.

Modules

  • Advanced Constitutional and Administrative Law (20 credits)
  • Criminal Law and Justice (20 credits)
  • Human Rights and International Standards (20 credits)
  • Legal Research and Writing (20 credits)
  • Contract and Tort Law (20 credits)
  • Advanced Legal Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Law (LLB) or related discipline OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma in Law with 2 years of experience OR
  • Relevant professional legal/paralegal background

Faculty : Faculty Of Law

Location :

  • Matara

Subject Area : Law

Duration : 36 Months

International Law

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in International Law is to provide learners with a deeper understanding of global legal systems, treaties, and international dispute resolution. It develops expertise in public and private international law, ensuring learners can navigate complex transnational legal issues.

Benefits

Learners will be equipped for roles such as International Legal Advisor, Diplomatic Service Officer, Policy Analyst, or Advocate in global law firms, with the option to progress to LLM or MBA pathways.

Modules

  • Principles of Public International Law (20 credits)
  • International Humanitarian and Human Rights Law (20 credits)
  • International Trade and Investment Law (20 credits)
  • International Arbitration and Dispute Resolution (20 credits)
  • Comparative Legal Systems (20 credits)
  • Advanced Legal Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Law, Political Science, or related field OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of international law/legal experience OR
  • Professional experience in diplomacy, policy, or international organizations

Faculty : Faculty Of Law

Location :

  • Colombo

  • Kandy

  • Matara

Subject Area : International Law

Duration : 36 Months

Business Law

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Business Law is to equip learners with advanced knowledge of corporate law, commercial transactions, and business dispute resolution. The programme focuses on aligning legal frameworks with corporate governance and strategic business operations.

Benefits

Graduates will be prepared for careers as Corporate Lawyers, Legal Advisors, Compliance Managers, or Policy Consultants, with pathways to LLM, MBA, or MSc qualifications.

Modules

  • Company and Corporate Law (20 credits)
  • Commercial and Contract Law (20 credits)
  • Employment and Labour Law (20 credits)
  • International Business Transactions (20 credits)
  • Corporate Governance and Compliance (20 credits)
  • Advanced Legal Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Law, Business, or related area OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of business/legal experience OR
  • Relevant professional/industry background

Faculty : Faculty Of Law

Location :

  • Colombo

  • Kandy

  • Matara

Subject Area : Business Law

Duration : 36 Months
