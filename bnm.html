Business Management

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Business Management is to develop learners’ knowledge and understanding of business operations and decision-making at a strategic level. The qualification focuses on equipping learners with the ability to critically analyze complex business problems, implement innovative solutions, and drive sustainable growth. It prepares learners for leadership roles across a wide range of business sectors.

Benefits

Successful completion of this qualification will enable learners to advance into senior management roles or progress to an MBA top-up with leading UK universities.

Modules

  • Strategic Management (20 credits)
  • Global Business Strategy (20 credits)
  • Strategic Marketing (20 credits)
  • Organizational Behaviour and Performance (20 credits)
  • Strategic Financial Management (20 credits)
  • Advanced Business Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of work experience OR
  • Managerial experience with relevant background

Faculty : Business And Management

Location :

  • Colombo

  • Kandy

  • Matara

Subject Area : Business Management

Duration : 12 Months

Human Resource Management (HRM)

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Human Resource Management is to enhance learners’ understanding of human capital development and strategic HR practices. The qualification provides in-depth knowledge of HR strategies, talent acquisition, employee relations, and performance management while aligning people management with organizational goals.

Benefits

This qualification equips learners with the expertise to become effective HR leaders and allows progression to MBA top-up programmes with UK universities.

Diploma in Entrepreneurship

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Entrepreneurship is to develop learners’ ability to innovate, create, and manage new business ventures or transform existing organizations. The qualification emphasizes entrepreneurial thinking, business planning, risk management, and sustainable innovation to prepare learners for high-level entrepreneurial and intrapreneurial roles.

Benefits

This qualification equips learners with the practical and theoretical foundations to start their own ventures or lead business transformation projects and offers progression to MBA/MA top-up programmes.

Modules

  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation (20 credits)
  • Strategic Business Planning (20 credits)
  • Global Business Environment (20 credits)
  • Strategic Leadership (20 credits)

  • Advanced Business Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of entrepreneurial/managerial experience OR
  • Demonstrated experience in business ownership or leadership

Faculty : Business And Management

Location :

  • Colombo

  • Kandy

  • Matara

Subject Area : Diploma in Entrepreneurship

Duration : 12 Months
