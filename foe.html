Civil Engineering

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Civil Engineering is to provide learners with advanced knowledge of structural design, construction management, and sustainable infrastructure development. The qualification equips learners to manage complex engineering projects and lead innovations in the built environment.

Benefits

Graduates will be prepared for roles such as Civil Engineer, Structural Engineer, or Project Manager and can progress to MSc or MBA top-up qualifications.

Modules

  • Advanced Structural Engineering (20 credits)
  • Sustainable Construction and Materials (20 credits)
  • Project and Construction Management (20 credits)
  • Geotechnical Engineering and Soil Mechanics (20 credits)
  • Transportation and Urban Infrastructure (20 credits)
  • Advanced Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Civil Engineering or related field OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of civil engineering experience OR
  • Relevant professional experience in construction/engineering
