Computer Science

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Computer Science is to provide learners with advanced theoretical and practical knowledge in computing and technology. The qualification focuses on problem-solving, innovation, and the application of modern computing methodologies to real-world challenges.

Benefits

Graduates will be equipped to pursue roles such as Software Architect, Systems Analyst, or IT Consultant and can progress to MSc/MBA top-up programmes.

Modules

  • Advanced Programming and Algorithms (20 credits)
  • Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (20 credits)
  • Advanced Database Systems (20 credits)
  • Strategic IT Management (20 credits)
  • Cloud Computing and Emerging Technologies (20 credits)
  • Advanced Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or related field OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of computing experience OR
  • Professional IT/technical experience

Cybersecurity

Objective

The objective of the OTHM Level 7 Diploma in Cybersecurity is to provide learners with specialist knowledge in securing digital infrastructures, preventing cyber threats, and ensuring data protection and resilience in organizations.

Benefits

Learners will be prepared for senior roles such as Cybersecurity Analyst, Security Architect, or Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and can progress to MSc/MBA programmes.

Modules

  • Advanced Cybersecurity Principles (20 credits)
  • Network and Cloud Security (20 credits)
  • Ethical Hacking and Penetration Testing (20 credits)
  • Risk Management and Cyber Law (20 credits)
  • Information Security Governance (20 credits)
  • Advanced Research Methods (20 credits)

Entry Qualification

  • Bachelor’s Degree in IT, Computer Science, or related area OR
  • Higher/Advanced Diploma with 2 years of cybersecurity/IT experience OR
  • Relevant professional industry certifications (e.g., CEH, CISSP)

Networking

Objective
